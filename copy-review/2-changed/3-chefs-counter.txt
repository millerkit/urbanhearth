Chef's Tasting Menu
Advance reservations required.

Price block
-----------
$190  per person, plus tax and tip
Bookings are paid in advance via OpenTable

Text on /dining-options (full version)
--------------------------------------
An intimate front-row seat to the creative heart of Urban Hearth.

Join us at our six-seat Chef’s Counter for a tasting menu of 8–12 courses
inspired by the season, the farm, and the larder. At the Counter, our chef
cooks and plates every course and talks you through each one as it arrives.

Led by Chef Erin Miller, a 2025 James Beard Award nominee for Best Chef:
Northeast, the menu showcases exceptional local ingredients, foraged
discoveries, and refined technique in a setting that is equal parts dinner
party and culinary performance.

The Chef’s Counter experience is available Tuesday through Saturday at 5:45
and 8:15 pm.

We offer an omnivore or vegetarian menu. Please note which you’d prefer in
your reservation. We can also accommodate the following dietary restrictions:
Pescatarian, Mammal-free, Nut-free, Shellfish-free, Gluten-free, Dairy-free.
If you have a combination of these or any other dietary restrictions, please
email concierge@urbanhearth.net before booking. Please note your dietary
restrictions and any other special requests in your reservation.
