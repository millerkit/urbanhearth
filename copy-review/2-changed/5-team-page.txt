Leadership Team  (/team)
========================
The people who make the restaurant what it is, every night.

Abby Taylor  —  General Manager
-------------------------------
Abby has spent 30 years in food and beverage, starting as a counter attendant
at a New England clam shack at 15, and has brought that same enthusiasm to
neighborhood favorites and Michelin-starred kitchens alike. As Urban Hearth's
General Manager since 2022, she has been a steady hand through the restaurant's
first James Beard nomination, its first Michelin recommendation, its Best of
Boston awards, and its move to Inman Square. She is devoted to providing a
supportive, sustainable work environment for our team and remarkable
hospitality for our guests.

Katie Janik  —  Beverage Manager
--------------------------------
Katie joined Urban Hearth in 2023 after a long career in hospitality that she
pursued, impressively, while putting herself through law school. Her
professionalism and composure are a constant on the floor, and her love of
wine is something guests get to benefit from directly. When she's not here,
she's working through a stack of books or drinking iced coffee, even in
January.

Rebecca Daulton  —  Sous Chef
-----------------------------
Originally from Columbus, Ohio, Rebecca left a career in accounting to pursue
what she actually loved: food. She joined Urban Hearth in 2022 as a kitchen
intern while studying classical French and Italian cuisine at the Cambridge
School of Culinary Arts, graduating as valedictorian in 2023. She considers it
the start of a lifelong culinary education. On the line she delights in
vegetables and the endlessly rewarding study of dough, both sweet and savory.
