À la Carte  (/menus/a-la-carte)
===============================

Intro line under the page title
-------------------------------
Shared plates from Erin Miller’s kitchen, from $8 to $56, changing with the
season. Order a few and pass them around.

Note under the intro
--------------------
Our menu evolves with the seasons. Items and prices are subject to change.
Please ask your server about today's complete offerings. Please note dietary
concerns in your reservation.

Walk-ins note
-------------
Walk-ins welcome; reservations recommended.
