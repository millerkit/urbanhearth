Our Story  (/about)
===================

Page text
---------
Urban Hearth is a Michelin-recommended, woman-owned restaurant in Inman
Square, Cambridge. Chef-owner Erin Miller's cuisine is seasonally driven,
built around close relationships with New England farmers, fishermen, and
foragers, and the belief that every dish carries a story of seasons, farmers,
memory, and place.

Erin opened Urban Hearth in 2016 as a 24-seat restaurant in North Cambridge.
In June 2026 it moved to a much larger space at 1281 Cambridge Street, in
Inman Square.

A 2025 James Beard Award nominee for Best Chef: Northeast, Erin approaches
fine dining as an act of generosity rather than exclusivity, and the
restaurant offers two ways in. The 32-seat dining room is à la carte shared
plates, and walk-ins are welcome. The six-seat Chef's Counter is the other
half: Erin cooks and plates every course and talks you through each one. One
is an open door, the other is the chef cooking for you personally, and both
come from the same idea.

The restaurant is built around the people who make it possible: the farmers,
fishermen, and foragers we source from; the kitchen team who compose each
plate; the dining room team who create the experience; and the guests who come
back season after season.
