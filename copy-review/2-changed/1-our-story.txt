Our Story  (/about)
===================

Page text
---------
Urban Hearth is a Michelin-recommended, woman-owned restaurant in Inman Square,
Cambridge. We cook to taste like New England: its seasons, its farms, its
coast. Our cuisine is built around close relationships with local farmers,
fishermen, and foragers, and the belief that every dish carries a story of
seasons, farmers, memory, and place.

Chef-owner Erin Miller opened Urban Hearth in 2016 as a 24-seat restaurant in
North Cambridge. In June 2026 it moved to a much larger space at 1281
Cambridge Street, in Inman Square.

We approach fine dining as an act of generosity rather than exclusivity, and
we offer two ways in. The 32-seat dining room is à la carte shared
plates, and walk-ins are welcome. The six-seat Chef's Counter is the other
half: our chef cooks and plates every course and talks you through each one.
One is an open door, the other is a chef cooking for you personally, and both
come from the same idea.

The restaurant is built around the people who make it possible: the farmers,
fishermen, and foragers we source from; the kitchen team who compose each
plate; the dining room team who create the experience; and the guests who come
back season after season.
