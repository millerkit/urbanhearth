Chef's Counter
==============

Text on /dining-options (full version)
--------------------------------------
An intimate front-row seat to the creative heart of Urban Hearth.

Join us at our six-seat Chef’s Counter for an immersive tasting menu featuring
8–12 courses inspired by the season, the farm, and the larder. Watch the
kitchen at work, engage directly with the chefs, and experience each dish as it
is finished and presented.

Led by James Beard Award-nominated Chef Erin Miller, the menu showcases
exceptional local ingredients, foraged discoveries, and refined technique in a
setting that is equal parts dinner party and culinary performance.

Thoughtful hospitality, lively conversation, and unforgettable food come
together in an experience designed to delight and nourish.

The Chef’s Counter experience is available Tuesday through Saturday at 5:45 and
8:15 pm.

We offer an omnivore or vegetarian menu. Please note which you’d prefer in your
reservation. We can also accommodate the following dietary restrictions:
Pescatarian, Mammal-free, Nut-free, Shellfish-free, Gluten-free, Dairy-free. If
you have a combination of these or any other dietary restrictions, please email
concierge@urbanhearth.net before booking. Please note your dietary restrictions
and any other special requests in your reservation.
