Chef's Counter
==============
Appears on /dining-options, /reservations and /menus/chefs-counter-menu

Label above the card (/menus/chefs-counter-menu)
------------------------------------------------
Chef's Tasting Menu
Advance reservations required.

Price block
-----------
$190  per person, plus tax and tip
Bookings are paid in advance via OpenTable, exclusions apply

Text on /dining-options (full version)
--------------------------------------
An intimate front-row seat to the creative heart of Urban Hearth.

Join us at our six-seat Chef’s Counter for an immersive tasting menu featuring
8–12 courses inspired by the season, the farm, and the larder. Watch the
kitchen at work, engage directly with the chefs, and experience each dish as
it is finished and presented.

Led by James Beard Award-nominated Chef Erin Miller, the menu showcases
exceptional local ingredients, foraged discoveries, and refined technique in a
setting that is equal parts dinner party and culinary performance.

Thoughtful hospitality, lively conversation, and unforgettable food come
together in an experience designed to delight and nourish.

The Chef’s Counter experience is available Tuesday through Saturday at 5:45
and 8:15 pm.

We offer an omnivore or vegetarian menu. Please note which you’d prefer in
your reservation. We can accommodate the following dietary restrictions:
Vegetarian, Pescatarian, Mammal-free, Nut-free, Shellfish-free, Gluten-free,
Dairy-free. If you have a combination of these or any other dietary
restrictions, please reach out before booking. Please note your dietary
restrictions and any other special requests in your reservation.
