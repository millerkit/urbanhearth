Erin Miller  (/chef)
====================
Executive Chef & Owner

Bio
---
Erin Miller is the Founder, Owner, and Chef of Urban Hearth, the nationally
recognized restaurant she opened in Cambridge, Massachusetts, in 2016. Under
her leadership, Urban Hearth has become one of Boston's most celebrated dining
destinations, earning recognition as a Michelin-recommended restaurant and
acclaim for its seasonal tasting menus, thoughtful hospitality, and deep
commitment to local sourcing.

A 2025 James Beard Award nominee for Best Chef: Northeast and participant in
the James Beard Foundation's Women's Entrepreneurial Leadership (WEL) program,
Erin is classically trained at the French Culinary Institute in New York and
honed her craft in acclaimed kitchens, including Dan Barber's Blue Hill. Her
cooking is guided by the seasons, shaped by close relationships with farmers,
fishermen, and foragers, and grounded in a belief that restaurants can be
powerful forces for both community and environmental stewardship.

Beyond the kitchen, Erin is passionate about mentorship and creating
opportunities for the next generation of hospitality professionals. She is
committed to building a restaurant culture that values curiosity,
craftsmanship, sustainability, and care for guests, employees, and the broader
food system alike.

Originally from the Blue Ridge Mountains of Virginia, Erin has called
Cambridge home for the past 20 years, where she lives with her husband and two
sons. When not in the kitchen, she spends time hiking, foraging, and
traveling. Her recent adventures include walking the length of Hadrian’s Wall
in England and biking the Rhone Valley with her son.
