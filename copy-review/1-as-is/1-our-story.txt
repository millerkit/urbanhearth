Our Story  (/about)
===================

Page text
---------
Urban Hearth is a Michelin-recommended, woman-owned restaurant in Inman
Square, Cambridge. Chef-owner Erin Miller's cuisine is seasonally driven,
built around close relationships with New England farmers, fishermen, and
foragers, and the belief that every dish carries a story of seasons, farmers,
memory, and place.

A James Beard Award nominee for Best Chef Northeast, Erin approaches fine
dining as an act of generosity rather than exclusivity. The restaurant is
built around the people who make it possible: the farmers, fishermen, and
foragers we source from; the kitchen team who compose each plate; the dining
room team who create the experience; and the guests who come back season after
season.
